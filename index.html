Welcome to After Hours

After Hours Tea Room is a cozy place to slow down, enjoy a cup of tea, and take a break from a busy day. Whether you are finishing school, leaving work, or simply looking for some quiet time, there is always a seat waiting for you.

Our Tea Selection

Our menu focuses on comforting teas with simple, familiar flavours. Try one of our signature choices:

  • Iced Chai Tea
  • Premium Green Tea
  • Bold Black Tea

Take your time, bring a friend, or enjoy a quiet moment by yourself. After Hours is all about making your everyday tea break feel a little more special.
